Jingang Zhou

I'm currently at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Previously, I pursued my master's studies at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science (AMSS), Chinese Academy of Sciences. I completed my undergraduate studies in Mathematics in the Shiing-Shen Chern Class (省身班) at Nankai University.

My research focuses on large language model post-training, on-policy distillation, model merging, and continual learning. I am interested in how learned capabilities can be transferred, combined, and continually improved.
